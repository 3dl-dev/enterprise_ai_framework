## What is being tested

Whether anyone wants an open, self-hosted enterprise AI control plane enough to say so unprompted.

Not whether it can be built. That is not in doubt and is not interesting.

## Signals, ranked by strength

Weight these when setting the threshold. The weak ones are listed so they are explicitly *not*
counted, not because they are useful.

| Strength | Signal | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Strongest | Someone from a distinct organisation describes their actual spend and current stack, unprompted | Costs them something to write; contains information only a real operator has |
| Strong | A third party disputes a specific design claim | They read it. Disagreement is engagement with substance |
| Strong | A third party runs a conformance suite against their own component | Adoption without telemetry — the phone-home-free adoption ledger |
| Moderate | Someone asks when it ships, or what would make it usable for them | Intent, but cheap to express |
| Weak | Issues from unknown domains, forks with a commit | Interest, not use |
| **Not counted** | Stars, watchers, traffic | Gameable, decoupled from production use, and we said we would not measure this |
